B. Traveling spiral waves mode
The characteristics of the traveling spiral waves mode is similar to those of the crossﬂow vortices
mode (stationary spiral waves mode), but the traveling spiral waves mode exhibits relative motion
with respect to the rotating disk. In the previous section, we analyzed and discussed the characteris-
tics of the crossﬂow vortices mode excited by roughness elements, identifying ﬂuctuations at higher
radii in the space-time plots of Figs. 8 and 9. Due to the lower Reynolds number, the rotating-disk
boundary layer did not undergo a transition to turbulence. To further analyze the role of roughness
elements in the transition to turbulence, the Reynolds number Re is increased to 3 × 10
5, which
corresponds to a maximumrδ of 547.7. At this condition, the roughness elements with a radiusr = 6
corresponds to rδ = 320.3. Despite the increase in the global Reynolds number Re, the variation in
the core rotation ratio vr (core) remains minimal and can still be considered as the critical Reynolds
number for current convective instability,rδc = 301.2. Given that the roughness elements are located
at a position where rδ > rδc , it is anticipated that disturbances will continue to grow.
