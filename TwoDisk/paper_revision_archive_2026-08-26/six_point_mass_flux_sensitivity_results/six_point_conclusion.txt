Interpretation
1. The sign structure is universal across the six tested points:
   S_b,i > 0, S_g,i < 0, and d omega_i/d a_s < 0.
2. Type-I sensitivity remains O(10^-2) at all three points. Its cancellation
   index remains in the narrow range 0.450--0.470.
3. Type-II sensitivity is weaker at all three points and becomes progressively
   smaller downstream because S_b,i and S_g,i approach equal magnitudes. Its
   cancellation index decreases from 0.157 at the critical point to 0.035 at
   R=350.
4. The same-radius comparison rules out radius alone as the explanation:
   at R=350 the Type-II net growth-rate sensitivity is only 1.77% of the
   Type-I value (the Type-I magnitude is about 56.5 times larger).
5. Therefore, the manuscript's qualitative conclusion is supported throughout
   the tested representative set: Type II has a much weaker net response
   because the base-flow and gradient contributions cancel more strongly.
   The numerical statement “about 14% of Type I” is local to the two critical
   points and must not be presented as a universal ratio.

Numerical validation
- N_cheb = 99, 129, and 199 give the same sensitivities to better than 1e-6
  in relative terms for the reported totals.
- delta a_s = 0.005, 0.01, and 0.02 preserve all signs and trends. The maximum
  finite-difference/adjoint relative error is 1.77e-4 at delta a_s=0.02,
  4.42e-5 at 0.01, and 1.11e-5 at 0.005, consistent with a second-order
  central difference.
- At N_cheb=199 and delta a_s=0.01, the minimum perturbed-mode overlap is
  0.999966, the maximum direct residual is 2.19e-11, and the maximum adjoint
  pairing error is 1.32e-14.

Important limitation
The tabulated Type-I critical point is rounded and gives omega_i =
4.89e-5 rather than exact neutrality; the Type-II value is 1.27e-6.
These are the two points already used in the manuscript. The six-point
comparison is internally converged, but a claim of evaluation at exact
criticality requires a separate continuous refinement of R, alpha, and beta.
The present test supports representative-point robustness at a_s=0; it does
not prove universality over all radii, wave numbers, Re_h, or nonzero a_s.
