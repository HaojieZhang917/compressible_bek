# Negative-Ro Fold Motion

This study uses the corrected traditional centrifugal Boussinesq coefficient
`Lambda_cf = Co^2/(4 Ro)`, with `Co=2-Ro-Ro^2`, and the fixed Pier map
`N=120, a=2, b=0.6, c=0.5`, `Pr=0.72`. The scan covers `Ro=-1.00` through
`-0.90` in steps of `0.01`; `Ro=0` is excluded because this Lingwood
differential-rotation scaling is singular there.

The production scan detected two folds at `Ro=-1`:
`(Hinf,Tw)=(-0.5327526,1.04802174)` and
`(-0.1133201,1.03014473)`. No extrema were detected for the sampled points
`Ro=-0.99,-0.98,...,-0.90`. This brackets a rapid fold-pair disappearance
between `-1` and `-0.99`, but does not by itself locate the cusp; a finer
bordered fold continuation is required.
